Privacy

Updated November 12, 2026. This is a small, independently run site for readers in the United States. It has no accounts, no forms and no newsletter. We do not ask for your name, e-mail or payment details. The site is run by Lucas Marques Arruda (Brazil); privacy questions: contato@iflux.space.

Where your visit came from

If you arrive from one of our ads, the page address carries a short campaign code, the search phrase the ad matched and Google's ad click identifier. The site keeps them in your browser's session storage until you close the tab and adds them to our paid link, so that a sale can be traced back to the ad that led to it. None of them contains your name or contact details. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the search phrase and click identifier are not kept or passed on.

Ad measurement

To learn which ads are worth paying for, the site may load Google's ad tag (Google Ads conversion tracking). It sets cookies so Google can tell us, in totals, how many ad visits led to a click on a paid link. Digistore24 may also report to us when a sale came from one of our ad clicks, using the click identifier; it does not send us your name, address or card details. Google handles this data under its own policy: how Google uses data from sites that use its services. When you click through to a seller

Our paid links take you to the seller's website by way of Digistore24. Digistore24 and the seller set their own cookies and, if you buy, process your order under their own privacy policies, not ours. They report sales to us with the product, the amount and the codes above. We never receive your name, address or card details.

Hosting and fonts

Our hosting provider receives standard technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver pages and keep the service secure. Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser type to deliver them.
